Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 May 2025  ·  Last updated: 3 May 2025

1. Who We Are

CourtStats OÜ ("we", "us", "our") is the developer of the CourtStats mobile application (the "App"), a racket sports score tracking tool for padel, tennis, beach tennis, and other racket sports.

CourtStats OÜ is a private limited company registered in Estonia, European Union.

Email: hello@courtstats.app
Website: courtstats.app

2. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use our App, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data. By using CourtStats, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Data You Provide Directly

When you use CourtStats, you may provide:

  • Your name (entered during onboarding to personalise the App)
  • Names of players you add to matches
  • Match data including scores, dates, and results

3.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Email addresses or passwords
  • Location or GPS data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, GAID, or similar)
  • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking data
  • Advertising data
  • Payment information (handled entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play)

3.3 Apple HealthKit Data (Optional — Elite Plan Only)

If you enable workout tracking as part of the Elite subscription, CourtStats may request access to Apple HealthKit to read and write heart rate, active calories, and workout duration. This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can revoke HealthKit access at any time via iPhone Settings.

4. How We Use Your Data

All data you enter in CourtStats is stored locally on your device only. We use your information solely to:

  • Display your match history and statistics within the App
  • Personalise your experience with your name
  • Provide core App functionality (score tracking, match history)

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or profiling. We do not sell your data to any third party.

5. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data you enter in CourtStats is stored locally on your device using Hive, a lightweight local database. Your data is never transmitted to CourtStats servers, never stored in the cloud, and never shared with third parties. CourtStats OÜ has no access to any data you enter in the App.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

Your data is stored locally on your device for as long as you have the App installed and have not deleted it. We do not impose any retention period.

6.2 How to Delete Your Data

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Within the App: Settings → Reset all data — permanently deletes all match history and your profile name
  • Uninstalling the App — removes all locally stored data from your device

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

7. Third-Party Services

7.1 Apple App Store and Google Play

CourtStats is distributed via the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. These platforms may collect certain data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7.2 In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

If you purchase a paid subscription (Stats or Elite), the transaction is processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. CourtStats OÜ does not collect or store any payment information.

7.3 AI Features (Elite Plan — Future)

The Elite subscription will include AI-powered match analysis features. When active, anonymised match data (scores, statistics — no personally identifiable information) may be sent to a third-party AI provider. We will update this Privacy Policy and seek your consent before enabling any AI data processing.

8. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

As CourtStats OÜ is registered in Estonia (EU), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing your data is contractual necessity — processing your name and match data is necessary to provide the App's core functionality you have requested.

9. Your Rights

As a user, and particularly as an EU/EEA resident, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — view all your data within the App at any time
  • Right to rectification — edit your name at any time via Settings → Profile
  • Right to erasure — delete all data via Settings → Reset all data or by uninstalling the App
  • Right to data portability — your data is stored locally and accessible on your device
  • Right to object — stop using the App and delete your data at any time

10. California Residents (CCPA)

CourtStats does not sell your personal information. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you already have direct access, control, and the ability to delete your data without contacting us. For any CCPA-related enquiries, please contact hello@courtstats.app.

11. Children's Privacy

CourtStats is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you have concerns, please contact us at hello@courtstats.app.

12. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends primarily on your device's security settings. We recommend keeping your device's operating system and the CourtStats App updated to the latest version.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and post a notice within the App. Your continued use of CourtStats after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
